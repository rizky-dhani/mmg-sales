# Initial Concept
A professional Healthcare Distribution CRM built with Laravel 12 and Filament 4, designed to streamline healthcare product distribution and manage sales relationships across the Java region.

# Product Guide: MMG Healthcare CRM

## Vision
To provide a robust, hierarchical CRM platform that empowers every level of the healthcare distribution sales force—from field representatives to executive leadership—with real-time data, streamlined workflows, and actionable insights.

## Target Audience
- **Operations (Super Admin):** System configuration, RBAC management, and master data integrity.
- **Executive Oversight (Board of Director):** Read-only access to company-wide data for strategic oversight without administrative or transactional capabilities.
- **Executive Leadership (Head):** High-level financial oversight and strategic distribution planning.
- **Regional Management (ASM/RSM):** Performance monitoring, regional hierarchy oversight, and pipeline analysis.
- **Field Sales (SR/SPV):** Daily activity logging, lead tracking, and order placement.

## Core Features
- **Hierarchical Dashboard:** Role-based analytics for sales performance (Net Sales, AOV) and CRM health.
- **Lead Pipeline Management:** Comprehensive tracking of healthcare facilities through the sales funnel.
- **Bulk Order Import:** Streamlined asynchronous Excel-based import system for bulk data entry with atomic validation.
- **Activity Audit Trail:** Detailed logging of calls, meetings, and demos to ensure accountability.
- **Geographic Distribution Tracking:** Focused management of territories within the Java region (Provinces/Cities).
- **Automated Inventory Insights:** Monitoring of products and segments with low-stock alerting.
- **Reporting Structure:** Built-in organizational hierarchy from Board level down to Sales Reps for seamless reporting and management lines.
