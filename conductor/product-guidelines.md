# Product Guidelines: MMG Healthcare CRM

## Prose Style & Brand Voice
- **Professional & Authoritative:** All communication within the system must convey reliability and precision. Use industry-standard medical contexts while maintaining a tone of expert authority.
- **Clarity Over Flair:** Prioritize accurate information delivery over marketing-style prose.

## Visual Identity & UI Design
- **Clinical Density:** The interface should blend a "clean and clinical" aesthetic (using trust-evoking medical blues and greens) with a "data-centric" density. 
- **Information Richness:** Optimize layouts to show high-value data at a glance, minimizing the need for excessive scrolling or clicking.
- **Trust & Precision:** Use high-contrast elements and purposeful white space to ensure data is legible and authoritative.

## Terminology & Naming
- **Simplified & User-Centric:** Avoid overly technical jargon where possible. Use terms that reflect the user's daily reality to reduce the learning curve (e.g., focusing on the practical use of a feature rather than its underlying technical name).

## Feedback & Notifications
- **Explicit Awareness:** Use immediate feedback mechanisms for all critical actions. 
- **Prominent Alerts:** Employ toast notifications and modal windows for success states, errors, and urgent events (like low-stock alerts) to ensure they are never overlooked.

## Device Strategy
- **Desktop-First:** Optimize primarily for the complex data management and dashboard needs of managers and administrators on desktop displays.
- **Responsive Adaptation:** Ensure core field sales tools (activity logging, customer lookup) are seamlessly adapted and highly functional on mobile and tablet devices for representatives on the move.
